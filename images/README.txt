HOW TO ADD PERSONALISED PHOTOS
================================
Place your images in this folder with the following filenames:

  sarah.jpg
  ali.jpg
  mom.jpg
  dad.jpg
  ahmed.jpg
  layla.jpg

The image will automatically appear in the greeting for the
matching ?name= URL parameter.

If a photo is not found the page still works — the photo section
is simply hidden. No broken images will show.

Recommended: Square images, at least 400×400px, JPG or PNG.
